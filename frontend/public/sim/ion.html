Detection window break it
Break it: shorten t_d — n̄_bright=R·t_d shrinks below the Poisson width √n̄, the bright & dark histograms overlap, and the readout fidelity drops. Selectivity is emergent from t_d·R vs √n̄, not hard-wired.
Rates (counts / unit time)
R = on-resonance 397 nm scatter rate × collection efficiency (the bright |g⟩ fluorescence R=Γ·ρ_ee).
R_bg = dark |e⟩ background / off-resonant rate (≈0).
Carrier drive (δ=0 → Rabi flop)
Rabi frequency Ω sets the flop rate; the period is 2π/Ω. A π-pulse (P_e=1) is t=π/Ω.
Detune off resonance → generalized Rabi √(δ²+Ω²): faster but shallower (contrast Ω²/(δ²+Ω²)).
Dephasing γ_φ break it
Raise γ_φ and the flops damp — coherence loss during the drive. At γ_φ=0 they oscillate forever.
Ramsey sequence (π/2 · T · π/2)
Detuning δ sets the fringe frequency: P_e(T)=½[1+cos(δT)]. Reading the fringe period measures δ.
Dephasing γ_φ → T₂* break it
Raise γ_φ and the fringe envelope collapses faster — a shorter coherence time T₂*≈1/γ_φ. This is how T₂* is measured.
